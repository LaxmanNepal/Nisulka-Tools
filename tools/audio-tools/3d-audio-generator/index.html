Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between 3D Audio and 8D Audio? ▼

3D audio creates the perception of sounds existing in three-dimensional physical space around you. "8D audio" is a popular creative music category where the stereo audio source continuously rotates in a 360° orbit around your head with added acoustic reverberation.

Why are headphones required for the 3D effect to work? ▼

The 3D binaural effect relies on HRTF (Head-Related Transfer Function) algorithms. Headphones feed each ear an isolated audio channel without room reflections or speaker cross-talk, allowing your brain to reconstruct full 360° spherical positioning.

Are my audio tracks uploaded to an external server? ▼

No. The entire conversion and rendering process occurs 100% locally inside your web browser using the HTML5 Web Audio API and OfflineAudioContext.

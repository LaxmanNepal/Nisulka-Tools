spatial_audio_off What is 3D Audio?

3D audio (also known as 8D audio when combined with reverb) is an auditory illusion created using spatial panning. It tricks your brain into thinking the sound is moving around you in a three-dimensional space, providing an immersive, concert-like experience right in your headphones.

Our tool utilizes advanced HRTF (Head-Related Transfer Function) algorithms to dynamically pan your audio, simulating a moving sound source.

How to Use

  1. Paste a YouTube video URL or 11-character Video ID into the input field.
  2. Choose your preferred thumbnail quality.
  3. Use Auto-Fetch for automatic loading while typing, or click Fetch manually.
  4. Preview the thumbnail and click Download Thumbnail to save it.
  5. Use Recent Thumbnails to quickly reopen thumbnails generated earlier on this device.

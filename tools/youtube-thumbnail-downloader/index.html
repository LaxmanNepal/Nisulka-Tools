What is this tool?

The YouTube Thumbnail Generator lets you extract the thumbnail image from any YouTube video in up to 4K quality. Simply paste a YouTube URL or video ID, choose your preferred quality, and download the thumbnail with one click.

All processing happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server, ensuring your privacy.

How to use

  1. Paste a YouTube video URL or Video ID into the input field.
  2. Select your preferred quality from the options (★ = premium).
  3. Toggle Auto-fetch to load thumbnails automatically as you type.
  4. Preview the thumbnail and click Download Thumbnail to save it.
  5. Access your recent thumbnails in the history section.

Supported formats

  • YouTube URLs — youtube.com, youtu.be, m.youtube.com
  • Video IDs — 11-character alphanumeric IDs
  • Quality options — Ultra HD, Full HD, High, Medium, Standard
  • Download — JPG format with descriptive filename

Privacy

This tool is 100% client-side. No data is sent to any server. Your YouTube URLs and thumbnails are processed entirely in your browser.

History and statistics are stored locally in your browser's storage and never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What YouTube thumbnail qualities are available?

We support five qualities: Ultra HD (1280×720), Full HD (640×480), High (480×360), Medium (320×180), and Standard (120×90). Premium qualities (marked with ★) may not be available for all videos — YouTube generates them based on video quality.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, the YouTube Thumbnail Generator is completely free. No registration, no hidden charges, and no usage limits.

Do you store my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your video URLs and thumbnails are never sent to any server. Local storage is used only for your history and stats.

Why is the Ultra HD quality not working for some videos?

YouTube only generates Ultra HD (maxresdefault) thumbnails for videos uploaded in high quality. Older or lower-resolution videos may not have this thumbnail available. Try selecting a lower quality option.

Can I use this for any YouTube video?

Yes, this tool works with any public YouTube video, including unlisted and private videos if you have the URL. Note that videos with age restrictions may have limited thumbnail availability.

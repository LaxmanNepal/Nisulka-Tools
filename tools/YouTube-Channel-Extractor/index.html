About YouTube Channel Analytics

This free tool helps you analyze any YouTube channel's performance metrics, including subscriber count, total views, video engagement, and growth patterns. Whether you're a content creator, marketer, or researcher, get valuable insights into channel performance.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Enter a YouTube Channel ID (starts with UC) or a username (starts with @)
  2. Select the search type (ID or Username)
  3. Click "Analyze" to fetch channel data
  4. Explore the different tabs for statistics, content, and insights
  5. Save channels for quick access later

Features

  • Channel statistics (subscribers, views, videos)
  • Content analysis with videos and shorts separation
  • Engagement metrics and performance ratings
  • Visual charts for performance and content distribution
  • AI-powered insights and recommendations
  • Save channels to your browser for quick access
  • Responsive design for all devices

Limitations

  • YouTube API has daily quota limits
  • Some channels may have restricted data access
  • Growth rates are estimated based on available data
  • Some metrics may not be available for all channels

Privacy

All data processing happens in your browser. Channel data is fetched directly from YouTube's public API. No data is stored on our servers. Saved channels are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my YouTube Channel ID?

You can find your Channel ID by going to your YouTube channel page, clicking on your profile picture, selecting "Settings" > "Advanced settings" or by using a Channel ID lookup tool.

What is the difference between Channel ID and Username?

A Channel ID is a unique identifier that starts with "UC" followed by a string of characters. A username (or handle) starts with "@" and is custom to the channel. Both can be used to look up a channel.

Are my searches private?

Yes. All searches are performed directly between your browser and YouTube's API. We do not store or process your search data on our servers.

Why can't I see all videos?

YouTube API limits the number of videos that can be fetched at once. Some older videos may not appear in the results.

What is the engagement rate?

Engagement rate is calculated as (likes + comments) / views × 100. It measures how engaged your audience is with the content.

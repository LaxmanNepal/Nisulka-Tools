About Gemini Watermark Remover

The Gemini Watermark Remover by Nisulka Tools is a specialized online utility designed to erase visible AI star icons, logos, and translucent text overlays created by Google Gemini and Imagen 3 AI image generators. Operating entirely within your browser using advanced Canvas Inpainting technology, your personal images are processed locally without being uploaded to any remote server.

How to Remove Watermarks from Gemini Generated Images

  1. Upload Your Image: Drag and drop your image file into the dropzone or click to browse.
  2. Select Watermark Region: Select the preset corner (most Gemini images place watermarks in the bottom-right corner) or paint over the watermark using the custom brush tool.
  3. Erase Watermark: Click "Remove Watermark Now" to execute local AI content-aware pixel inpainting.
  4. Download Clean Result: Compare the before/after preview and download your clean image in PNG, JPG, or WebP format.

Key Features

  • Client-Side Processing: Complete privacy. Images never leave your device.
  • Auto-Preset Corners: Pre-configured targeting for Gemini, Imagen 3, Midjourney, and DALL-E default watermark positions.
  • Precision Inpainting Brush: Custom brush tool to highlight specific or irregular watermarks easily.
  • Multiple Export Options: Save your cleaned images in original high resolution in PNG, JPG, or WebP formats.

Supported Formats & Limits

Supports all major web image formats including JPG, PNG, and WebP up to 25 MB per file. Works seamlessly on modern browsers on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does this tool upload my images to a server?

No. All pixel processing and watermark inpainting happen locally on your device using Web API Canvas technology. Your files are never transmitted across the internet.

Where does Google Gemini place watermarks on images?

Google Gemini and Imagen 3 typically insert a small four-pointed star logo in the bottom-right or bottom-left corner of generated images.

Can I remove custom watermarks or logos on other AI tools?

Yes. By choosing "Custom Brush Selection", you can draw over any watermark, text overlay, or unwanted artifact anywhere on your image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this whiteboard tool free to use?

Yes, Nisulka Whiteboard Canvas is 100% free for unlimited personal, educational, and commercial use.

Are my drawings saved on a server?

No, all drawings are processed locally on your browser. No drawing data is stored on remote servers, ensuring complete privacy.

Can I export my whiteboard drawings?

Yes! Click the Export button in the toolbar to save your current canvas layout as a PNG image.

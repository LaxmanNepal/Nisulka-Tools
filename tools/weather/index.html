Understanding Weather Metrics

Temperature Scale

Ambient temperature is recorded in shaded, ventilated enclosures. Easily toggle between Celsius (°C) and Fahrenheit (°F) at any time.

Wind & Air Quality

Wind speeds reflect atmospheric dynamics, while AQI measures ground-level ozone, nitrogen dioxide, and particulate matter.

UV Radiation

The Ultraviolet Index measures UV solar radiation levels from 0 (Low) to 11+ (Extreme), helping you prepare for safe outdoor activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How frequently is the weather forecast data updated?

Our weather data updates continuously using live meteorological observation API feeds from OpenWeatherMap, ensuring real-time accuracy for temperature, wind, air quality, and precipitation forecasts.

Can I detect weather based on my current location?

Yes! Simply click the "Use Current Location" button in the search panel to grant browser geolocation access and fetch localized weather metrics instantly.

How is the 24-hour timeline generated?

The 24-hour timeline extracts sequential 3-hour atmospheric measurement intervals across the immediate 24-hour forecast window.

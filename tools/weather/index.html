Understanding Meteorological Telemetry

Solar Radiation & UV Index

The UV Index quantifies solar ultraviolet radiation intensity. Levels 0-2 present minimal risk, 3-5 moderate risk, while 6+ requires protective clothing and sunscreen.

Air Quality Index (AQI)

Tracks fine particulate matter (PM2.5, PM10) and toxic gases. Scores under 50 signify pristine air, whereas scores above 100 suggest caution for sensitive groups.

Barometric Pressure Trends

Atmospheric pressure fluctuations indicate weather transitions. Dropping pressure signals incoming rain or storm fronts, whereas rising pressure brings fair skies.

Frequently Asked Questions

How frequently is the weather data updated?

The meteorological telemetry is sourced directly from Open-Meteo observational endpoints, updating hourly with live satellite and radar synchronization.

How is the Moon Phase computed?

The lunar phase is computed algorithmically based on Julian day offsets and the synodic lunar cycle (29.53 days), accurately identifying new, quarter, and full moons.

Can I switch between Celsius and Fahrenheit?

Yes! Simply click the unit toggle button in the header bar or settings panel to switch temperatures and wind speeds seamlessly across all charts.

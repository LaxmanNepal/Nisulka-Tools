Understanding Real-Time Currency Conversion

What is the Mid-Market Rate?

The mid-market rate (also called the spot rate or interbank rate) is the midpoint between the buy and sell prices of two currencies in global markets. It represents the purest, fairest exchange rate without added bank margins.

Why Do Bank Rates Differ?

Traditional banks, retail exchange outlets, and credit cards add hidden markups (usually 2% to 5%) on top of the real mid-market rate. Our tool provides the live benchmark rate so you can evaluate foreign exchange fees.

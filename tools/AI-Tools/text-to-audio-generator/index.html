Frequently Asked Questions

What languages does this AI Voice Generator support?

How do tone and expression tags work?
You can select expression modifiers like Cheerful or Soft Whisper, or insert inline tags like [Pause 1s] to add natural inflection and pauses.
How do audio filters (Radio, Reverb, Bass Boost) work?
Our built-in Web Audio DSP engine applies real-time acoustic equalization and reverberation filters directly inside your browser.

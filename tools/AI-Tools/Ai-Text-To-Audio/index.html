About the Voice AI Toolkit

The Voice AI Toolkit allows you to turn text into clear, downloadable audio files (.wav) or real-time speech synthesis. Powered by high-definition AI neural voices, you can generate natural voiceovers, podcasts, dramatic narrations, or transcribe audio into text instantly.

HD AI Audio Export (.WAV)

Synthesize high-fidelity voice tracks and download lossless WAV audio files directly to your browser for video editing, podcasts, or voiceover production.

Expressive Voice Direction

Select emotional tones including dramatic storytelling, cheerful, calm meditation, whisper, or professional broadcast modes.

Multi-Speaker Conversations

Format scripts with dialogue prefixes (e.g., Joe: ... / Jane: ...) to generate multi-speaker interviews and podcast conversations.

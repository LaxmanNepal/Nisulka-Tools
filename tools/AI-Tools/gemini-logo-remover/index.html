Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript and Canvas. Your image never leaves your device.

How does the watermark removal work?

It uses reverse alpha blending. Gemini applies its logo with a known alpha map; we invert the blend mathematically to recover the original pixels.

What image formats are supported?

PNG, JPEG, and WebP are supported. The output is always a PNG to preserve quality.

Will the result be pixel-perfect?

Yes, when the watermark matches the known alpha maps. We auto-detect the correct map based on image dimensions.

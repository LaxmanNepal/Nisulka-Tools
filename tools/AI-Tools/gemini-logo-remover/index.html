How Gemini Watermark Removal Works

When Google's Imagen 3 or Gemini models synthesize an image, a small semi-transparent watermark logo is blended into the corner pixels. Unlike traditional generative inpainting methods that hallucinate or blur pixels, this tool performs pure Reverse Alpha Blending.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool reduce the quality of my image?
No! Because this tool uses reverse alpha mathematics rather than lossy AI inpainting, the unaffected pixels remain 100% identical, and watermarked pixels are perfectly reconstructed.
Is my image uploaded to any server?
Never. Nisulka Tools processes all image pixel data directly in your browser's memory using JavaScript and WebGL/Canvas APIs.
Where is the Gemini watermark located?
By default, Gemini places the watermark logo in the bottom-right corner. You can adjust the position setting if your image has been flipped or cropped.

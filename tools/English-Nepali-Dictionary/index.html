Resilient

ADJECTIVE
/rɪˈzɪl.jənt/ • रिजिलिएन्ट
Primary Nepali Translation (नेपाली अर्थ):

लचिलो, सहनशील, कठिनाइबाट चाँडै तंग्रिने

Definition & Sense

  • Able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions or adversity.
  • Returning to an original shape or position after being bent, stretched, or compressed.

Contextual Examples (उदाहरणहरू)

"She showed a resilient spirit despite facing many personal challenges."

"धेरै व्यक्तिगत चुनौतीहरूको सामना गर्दा पनि उनले सहनशील भावना देखाइन्।"

"Nepali people are known to be remarkably resilient after natural disasters."

"प्राकृतिक विपत्तिपछि नेपाली जनता असाधारण रूपमा सहनशील हुने भनेर चिनिन्छन्।"

Synonyms (समानार्थी)

tough adaptable strong buoyant

Antonyms (विपरितार्थी)

fragile vulnerable weak

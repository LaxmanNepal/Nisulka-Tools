Why Optimize & Compress Your Images?

Images constitute a substantial portion of average website page weight. Large, uncompressed images slow down page load times, increase bandwidth usage, and create a frustrating user experience, especially on mobile networks.

By compressing your images prior to publishing them on your blog, ecommerce shop, or web app, you can easily reduce file sizes by 50% to 80% without noticeable degradation in human-perceived visual quality. Fast-loading websites rank higher on Google Search, maintain lower bounce rates, and convert better.

Choosing the Right Format: JPEG vs. PNG vs. WebP

  • WebP: The recommended next-gen standard for web developers. Supports both lossy and lossless compression with transparency, yielding up to 30% smaller files than JPEG.
  • JPEG / JPG: Excellent choice for complex photographs and detailed graphics. Offers variable lossy compression to balance size and sharpness.
  • PNG: Best for line art, screenshots, logos, and images requiring crisp text or transparent backgrounds. Note that PNG utilizes lossless compression, so size reduction depends on color complexity.
